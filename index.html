How it works

The idea is simple: a senior has a home with room to share. A caregiver needs housing and wants meaningful work. TEST helps them find each other safely and puts a clear agreement around it.

  1. Tell us about yourself

    Seniors and caregivers each fill out a short profile — what kind of help is needed, what kind of home is offered, and what matters most to you.

  2. We screen and match

    Caregivers go through background checks and interviews. We suggest matches based on needs, personality, and location — and you meet before anything is decided.

  3. Agree on the arrangement

    Everyone signs a clear, plain-language agreement covering the help provided, the housing offered, and how either side can end the arrangement.

  4. Live with ongoing support

    TEST stays involved with check-ins and support for both sides — this isn't a listing site that disappears after the introduction.

Built around trust

Inviting someone into a home — or moving into someone else's — takes real trust. That's why safety comes before matching, every time.

  • ✓

Common questions

Is this the same as a home care agency?

No. A home care agency sends paid staff to your home for scheduled visits. TEST matches you with one caregiver who lives with you and helps out as part of a housing arrangement. It's closer to having a trusted housemate who helps than to hiring hourly staff.

What kind of help do caregivers provide?

Everyday, non-medical help: cooking, errands, light housekeeping, rides, reminders, and — just as important — companionship. Caregivers are not a replacement for nurses or medical care.

How are caregivers checked?

Every caregiver goes through background checks, reference checks, and interviews before we introduce them to anyone. You (and your family, if you like) meet them in person before deciding anything.

What does it cost?

The arrangement is built on exchanging housing for help, which is why it can cost much less than a care facility or full-time hired care. Exact costs depend on each arrangement — we'll walk you through the details before you commit to anything.

What if it doesn't work out?

Every agreement includes a clear, fair way for either side to end the arrangement. TEST stays involved after the match, so if something isn't working, you have someone to call.
